# Sample warehouse handbook

## Picking orders
Pick boxes in aisle order. Scan the product label before putting a box on the cart. If stock is short, record the missing quantity and tell the shift lead. An order can be marked ready only after every requested box has been picked.

## Loading orders
Check the order number at the loading bay. Count the boxes on the cart and compare that count with the pick list. Mark an order loaded only after the boxes are placed on the correct vehicle.

## Stock changes
A manager must approve each stock adjustment. The worker should record the product, the quantity change, and the reason for the change. Stock must never fall below zero.

## Damaged boxes
Move damaged boxes to the marked hold area. Do not include damaged boxes in a customer order. Tell the shift lead and request a stock adjustment.

## Delivery windows
The morning delivery window is 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM. The afternoon delivery window is 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM. Call the receiving desk before arriving outside these windows.
